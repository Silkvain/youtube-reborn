Reborn

What this shares

  • Once a day: which public videos you watched (the day, never the time) and roughly how much of each.
  • Once a week: pairs of channels you watch a lot, like “someone watches both A and B”.

What it never shares

  • Who you are: no account, ID or cookie. Each upload uses a one-time anonymous token, so uploads can’t be linked to each other or to you.
  • Anything you watched before turning this on, your searches, or anything outside YouTube.

Only totals that many people contributed to are published, with random noise added. Turn it off any time: sharing stops at once and downloaded data is deleted.

Turn off Enhanced discoverability?

Reborn will stop sharing, and the community data on this device is deleted: “Popular with viewers of …” picks, community picks in the top row, and the shared upload pool. Your own feed keeps working. You can turn it back on later.

Everything is stored on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere except youtube.com.
