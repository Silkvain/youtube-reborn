Welcome to Reborn

Reborn brings back the old YouTube recommendations: “people who watch this also watch that”, built from what real viewers watch, not from what keeps you scrolling.

For that, Reborn needs Enhanced discoverability switched on

Recommending the way old YouTube did means knowing what people watch. With your permission, Reborn collects which YouTube videos you watch and shares them anonymously with the Reborn server. Everyone’s anonymous totals power everyone’s recommendations, so the more people join, the better the recommendations get.

What it shares

  • Once a day: which public videos you watched (the day, never the time) and roughly how much of each.
  • Once a week: pairs of channels you watch a lot, like “someone watches both A and B”.

What it never shares

  • Who you are: no account, ID or cookie. Each upload uses a one-time anonymous token, so uploads can’t be linked to each other or to you.
  • Anything you watched before turning this on, your searches, or anything outside YouTube.

Only totals that many people contributed to are published, with random noise added. If you deny, nothing is shared: you still get a private feed built on this device from your subscriptions and the channels they feature, but not the community recommendations Reborn is built around. You can change your mind any time with the Enhanced discoverability switch in the toolbar popup; switching it off stops sharing at once and deletes the downloaded data.
